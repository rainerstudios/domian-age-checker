Privacy Policy for Domain Age Checker

Last updated: September 5, 2025

Introduction

Domain Age Checker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how our Chrome extension collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

Information We DO NOT Collect:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information
  • We do not track your browsing history
  • We do not store any data about the domains you check
  • We do not use cookies or local storage for tracking
  • We do not collect email addresses, names, or any personal identifiers

Information Processing:

  • Domain names are sent to third-party APIs only for the purpose of retrieving domain age information
  • This information is processed temporarily and not stored by our extension
  • API requests are made directly from your browser to the domain information service

How We Use Information

The Domain Age Checker extension:

  • Only accesses the current tab's URL when you open the extension popup
  • Extracts the domain name for the sole purpose of checking its age
  • Makes API requests to retrieve publicly available domain registration information
  • Displays this information to you within the extension popup

Third-Party Services

Our extension uses third-party domain information APIs to retrieve domain age data. These services may have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party services.

Currently used API services:

  • Domain API services for retrieving WHOIS information

Data Security

Since we do not collect or store any personal data, there is minimal security risk. All domain lookups are performed in real-time and no data is retained by our extension.

Permissions

Our extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • activeTab: To read the current tab's URL for automatic domain checking

We use minimal permissions and only access what is necessary for the extension's functionality.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our extension, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store or our GitHub repository.
